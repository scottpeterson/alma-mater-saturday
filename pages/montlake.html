The subscriptions that matter

You watch from outside the Seahawks' home market, so the regional Sunday-afternoon games need Sunday Ticket. National broadcasts and every Husky game work anywhere.

NFL Sunday Ticket

The main service for out-of-market fans. It carries every regional Sunday-afternoon FOX and CBS game, through YouTube or YouTube TV. Without it, most Seahawks Sunday games do not air in West Michigan.

FOX One

FOX, FS1, FS2, and Big Ten Network (BTN), including your live local FOX station. It covers the national FOX games (the Saturday Eagles game, Christmas vs. the Rams) and every Husky game on FOX, FS1, or BTN. Prices: $19.99/mo or $199.99/yr, $39.99/mo bundled with ESPN Unlimited, or $15/mo as a Verizon myPlan or myHome perk. The B1G+ add-on covers only events that are not on TV, so you don't need it for football.

Peacock

NBC games: the Wednesday-night opener, Sunday Night Football vs. Kansas City, the Apple Cup, and Washington at USC on Big Ten Saturday Night.

ESPN Unlimited

Both Monday Night Football home games: Bears in November, Cowboys in December.

Prime Video

One game only: Thursday night at Denver in Week 6. NFL+ streams every primetime game on phones and tablets, so you can watch that game without Prime.

NFL+ Premium

The best value for a Seahawks fan outside Seattle. It includes:

  • Live out-of-market preseason games (both KING 5-only broadcasts)
  • Every primetime game live on a phone or tablet, including Sunday Night Football, Monday Night Football, and Prime's Thursday Night Football
  • Full and condensed replays of every game, including the regional Sunday games that only Sunday Ticket carries live
  • NFL RedZone and NFL Network

Live games play only on phones and tablets, with no casting. Replays play on smart TVs and streaming boxes. $14.99/mo or $99.99/yr. The 20% annual discount (about $80) ended September 21.

Grand Rapids is its own TV market. The local FOX (WXMI 17) and CBS (WWMT 3) stations usually carry the Lions' window, so Seattle's regional Sunday games rarely air there. If a network puts the Seahawks in the national doubleheader window, the game is free over the air. Check the weekly coverage maps at 506sports.com before each Sunday.
Husky games marked TBD (to be determined) air on FOX, CBS, NBC, BTN, or Peacock under the Big Ten's TV contract. If CBS picks a game, you need Paramount+. College broadcasts are national, so blackouts do not apply. The conference announces each kickoff time and network about 12 days before the game.
A pricing plan at full price (no introductory trials):
  • Keep Peacock year-round.
  • Buy NFL+ Premium annual at $99.99. The September discount to about $80 ended September 21.
  • Add FOX One ($19.99/mo) only for the months with games you care about, and cancel it between them.
  • Skip Sunday Ticket unless live regional Sunday games matter more to you than replays. NFL+ Premium has a full replay of each of those games.
