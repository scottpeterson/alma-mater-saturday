Where to watch

Every network and stream that appears on one of the five schedules this season, with what you need to watch it. Subscription prices are the published 2026-27 prices and can change.

BTN (Big Ten Network)

SubscriptionCable channelOn a schedule now

Teams: Washington, Indiana

Pay-TV subscription that carries Big Ten Network. That means cable or satellite, or a live-TV streaming service such as YouTube TV, Hulu + Live TV, Sling (Blue), Fubo, or DirecTV Stream. Fox One, Fox's own streaming service, also carries BTN.

B1G+ is a separate subscription for events BTN does not televise. It does not carry BTN's football games.

FOX (FS1)

FreeBroadcast networkOn a schedule now

Teams: Washington, Indiana

Free over the air with an antenna. Also on every pay-TV and live-TV streaming service, and on Fox One.

FOX games are over the air. FS1 games need a pay-TV or live-TV streaming subscription. Big Noon Saturday is FOX's noon ET window.

Local stations: FOX 17 (WXMI) in the Grand Rapids area and FOX 32 (WFLD) in the Chicago area.

NBC

FreeBroadcast networkOn a schedule now

Teams: Washington, Indiana

Free over the air with an antenna. Streams live on Peacock Premium and on the live-TV streaming services.

Big Ten Saturday Night is NBC's prime-time window. NBC games also stream on Peacock, but Peacock-only games do not air on NBC.

Local stations: WOOD TV8 in the Grand Rapids area and NBC 5 (WMAQ) in the Chicago area.

Peacock

SubscriptionStreaming serviceOn a schedule now

Teams: Washington, Indiana

Peacock subscription (Premium or Premium Plus). No pay-TV package includes it.

A few Big Ten games each season stream only on Peacock.

CBS (Paramount+)

FreeBroadcast network

Teams: Washington, Indiana

Free over the air with an antenna. Streams live with Paramount+ Premium (the tier that includes your local CBS station) and on the live-TV streaming services.

CBS has the Big Ten's 3:30 PM ET window on most Saturdays.

Local stations: WWMT 3 in the Grand Rapids area and CBS 2 (WBBM) in the Chicago area.

CCIW Network

FreeConference streamOn a schedule now

Teams: Wheaton (IL)

Free. No account or subscription. Watch at cciwnetwork.com on desktop or mobile.

Launched in 2025 with Hudl as the platform. Covers CCIW home games, including non-conference home games, and CCIW championships.

MIAA Digital Network (MIAA Sports Network)

Price not publishedConference streamOn a schedule now

Teams: Hope, Calvin

MIAA Sports Network is the Michigan Intercollegiate Athletic Association's official stream. It runs on Hudl TV at miaasportsnetwork.com and in the MIAA Sports Network app for Apple TV, Roku, Fire TV, Android TV, iOS, and Android. The app is free to install. As of October 3, 2026, the network lists no viewing price.

The Michigan MIAA is a Division III conference. The Mid-America Intercollegiate Athletics Association (Division II) runs a different MIAA Network, which charges $15 per broadcast or $140 per year.

YouTube (Calvin Broadcast Network)

FreeSchool streamOn a schedule now

Teams: Calvin, Hope, Wheaton (IL)

Free. Many Division III schools, including Calvin, stream home games on their own YouTube channel.

Calvin Broadcast Network is Calvin's student-run broadcast. The game link appears on the school's schedule page a few hours before kickoff.

FloSports (FloCollege)

SubscriptionStreaming serviceOn a schedule now

Teams: Calvin (one road game)

FloCollege subscription: $24.99 per month or $119.88 per year for 2026-27, with a discount for .edu addresses from partner-conference schools.

FloSports holds streaming rights for many Division II and III conferences. A game streams here when the host school's conference is a FloSports partner.

North Coast Network

FreeConference streamOn a schedule now

Teams: Hope (one road game)

Free. The North Coast Athletic Conference's stream, run with BlueFrame, is available on the web and in iOS, Apple TV, Roku, and Fire TV apps.

Team 1 Sports (Hanover Sports Network)

FreeSchool streamOn a schedule now

Teams: Hope (one road game)

Free. Hanover College's stream, on the web and through the Team1Sports app on Apple TV, Roku, Fire TV, and Android TV.
